FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our packaging, retail and wholesale ordering, delivery options, and payment.

Both Laxmi Groundnut Oil and Laxmi Cottonseed Oil are standardly available in three pack formats: 15 kg Tin (for commercial kitchens, heavy frying, and bulk household needs), 5 L Can (handy molded container for regular household consumption), and 1 L Bottle (ergonomic bottle for daily culinary use). Sunflower Oil and other edible varieties are also stocked in popular sizes.
We proudly cater to both! Individual walk-in household customers can purchase single 1-liter bottles or 5-liter cans directly at our depot counter. At the same time, we operate a dedicated wholesale wing for caterers, restaurants, and retail resellers ordering multiple 15-kg tins.
Local delivery arrangements are available for eligible bulk and wholesale orders within our operational distribution territory. For retail customers and immediate requirements, direct self-pickup from our depot shop is always available during regular business hours.
Ordering is straightforward:
  1. Walk-in: Visit our depot shop during open hours and pick up your desired oils on the spot.
  2. Phone or WhatsApp: Call or text us with your oil type, pack size, and volume to reserve stock.
  3. Online Enquiry Form: Fill out the inquiry form below, and our team will get in touch with rate details and fulfillment scheduling.
We accept cash payments at our counter, direct UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and bank transfers for authorized wholesale commercial invoices.
